outcomes:
- Find your way around the FreeCAD interface and move around a 3D model
- Build simple models from Part primitives such as boxes, cylinders, spheres and cones
- Combine and cut shapes with Fuse, Cut and Common, and soften edges with fillets
  and chamfers
- Understand how assemblies and technical drawings are made from finished parts
- Check a model and export it as STL or STEP for 3D printing and sharing
prerequisites:
- No CAD experience needed, just basic computer skills such as saving files and using
  a mouse
- Helpful but not required, Building SMARS with FreeCAD as the next project course
you_need:
- A computer running Windows, macOS or Linux (a Raspberry Pi 4 or 5 works for the
  basics)
- Permission to install software, or a school machine that already has FreeCAD
- A mouse with a scroll wheel, which makes 3D navigation much easier
- Optional, a 3D printer or print service to turn your STL files into real parts
