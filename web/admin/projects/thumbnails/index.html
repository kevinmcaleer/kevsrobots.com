Models uploaded in the editor get a thumbnail as they upload. These are the models that have none yet. Rendering happens here in your browser, one model at a time, and each thumbnail is saved as soon as it is drawn. OBJ, 3MF, STEP and G-code files can't be drawn yet, so they stay on the list.
