This pathway takes one robot all the way from an idea to a machine that moves, using two free tools that work together: [BetterCad](https://bettercad.kevsrobots.com) for the body and [Snakie](https://app.snakie.org) for the wiring and the code. Both run in your browser.

It follows the order you'd work in on a real build:

1. **Design.** Learn BetterCad, then design parts around real servos, boards and bearings
2. **Print.** Check, calibrate and export so the parts fit first time
3. **Joint.** Give the parts hinges, pose them, check for clashes and export the robot as URDF
4. **Wire.** Find your way round Snakie, then plan and check the electronics before you pick up a jumper wire
5. **Code and animate.** Bring the robot into Snakie's Build workspace, bind its servos and play back poses on the real thing
6. **Give it a face.** Finish with a pair of animated eyes on an OLED screen

The desk arm you joint in BetterCad is the same arm you animate in Snakie, so your work carries over from one course to the next.
