New to [Snakie](https://app.snakie.org), the robotics-first MicroPython editor? Start here. You'll find your way around the app, program a Raspberry Pi Pico by snapping blocks together, and then learn the MicroPython behind them. Most of it runs on Snakie's built-in simulator, so you can begin before you own a board.
