New to [Snakie](https://app.snakie.org), the robotics-first MicroPython editor? Start here. You'll find your way around the app, program a Raspberry Pi Pico by snapping blocks together, and learn the MicroPython behind them. Then you'll plan and check a robot's wiring in the Electronics workspace, and use the oscilloscope, multimeter and plotter to see what your code is really doing. Most of it runs on Snakie's built-in simulator, so you can begin before you own a board.
