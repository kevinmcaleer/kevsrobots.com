# Privacy Policy
This is the Privacy Policy page for kevsrobots.com

---

## Nibsy's suggestions

Nibsy, the robot helper in the corner of each page, suggests other pages you might like.

- **If you are not logged in**, Nibsy's suggestions are based only on the page you are looking at. Nothing about what you read is stored against you.
- **If you are logged in**, Nibsy remembers which articles, courses and reviews you have opened (the page, when you last opened it and how many times) so it can suggest things that fit your interests. This is kept for 90 days, is used only to choose suggestions on this site, and is never shared.

You can erase it at any time: click Nibsy and choose **Forget What I've Read**.
